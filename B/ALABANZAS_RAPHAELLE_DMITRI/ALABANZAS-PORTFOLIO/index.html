01 / About

I like simple things that hide interesting ideas.

My projects tend to start with a question: can this be made clearer, more human, more useful, or just more fun? That question has led me through frontend development, Firebase-backed apps, computer vision, HCI studies, creative communications, and storytelling.

The goal is not to make everything loud. It is to make the important part visible — a useful interaction, a strong visual, a memorable line, or a small detail that makes someone go, “okay, that feels right.”
