About

I trained as a product designer, then spent a weekend course on a wheel and never went back. I now throw about 12 hours a week and spend another 10 on glazing, packing and answering email.

Most of my clay is a local grey stoneware. I mix my own celadon and ash glazes, which is why no two batches match exactly.
