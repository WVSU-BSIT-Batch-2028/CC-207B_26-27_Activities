Project: Personal Finance Tracker

An individual project, planned, designed, and built by me.

The problem

Tracking money in notes or from memory is easy to forget and hard to review. PennyWise gives students a simple place to log income and expenses and see where their money goes.

My role

Solo: I set the scope, scheduled the work, designed the screens for both users and an admin, and built the app.

Process

I separated the app into a user side and an admin side, planned each screen around one clear task, then built features one at a time, from sign-up and login to charts and transaction editing.

For Users

  • Dashboard with total balance, income, and expenses at a glance
  • Spending trend (last 7 days) and monthly expenses by category charts
  • Quick transaction entry with category, date, and note
  • Filter recent transactions by all, income, or expense
  • Double-click a transaction to edit or delete it

For Admins

  • Overview of registered, active, and recent users
  • User management to view and remove accounts
  • Customizable income and expense categories
  • Login logs for transparency and security

What I learned

Clear screens and instant feedback, like confirmation messages and charts that update right away, make an app easier to trust. A written plan also keeps a solo project on schedule.
