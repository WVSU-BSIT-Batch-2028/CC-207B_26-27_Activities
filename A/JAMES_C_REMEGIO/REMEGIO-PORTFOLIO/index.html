02 / ABOUT

A builder who treats leadership like engineering.

James C. Remegio is a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student at West Visayas State University and currently serves as a University Student Councilor for A.Y. 2026–2027. He is passionate about software development, robotics, research, and using technology to solve real-world problems.

His leadership includes serving as Board Member for Research and Development of the Cyb Robotics Organization, Assistant Secretary of the CICT Student Council, and Vice Chairperson of the CICT Junior Student Council.

Beyond student governance, he placed 1st in RealiTy XIII: METROPOLIS — IDEATHON, organized by WVSU Link.exe, and became champion of a National Robotics Competition held in Pampanga in 2020.
