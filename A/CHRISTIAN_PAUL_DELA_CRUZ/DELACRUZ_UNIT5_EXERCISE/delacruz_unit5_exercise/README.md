# Christian Paul De la Cruz — Unit 5 Exercise

A two-screen Flutter profile and dog screen. The Profile screen shows student
information and a biography; the My Pet screen demonstrates dog-bark audio
playback, a play counter, and a double-tap favorite toggle. Dogs are Christian's
favorite animal; the photo and bark can be replaced with his own dog media.

## Run

```sh
flutter pub get
flutter run
```

## Personalize

- Update the name, tagline, biography, and `profileInfos` values in
  `lib/main.dart`.
- Replace `assets/images/profile_illustration.jpg` with your own portrait and
  update its semantic label.
- Replace `assets/images/favorite_dog.jpg` and
  `assets/sounds/dog_bark.wav` with a favorite animal's image and sound.
  Update the animal name and sound label in `lib/main.dart` to match.
- The profile lists the year and section as `BSIT 3A`.
